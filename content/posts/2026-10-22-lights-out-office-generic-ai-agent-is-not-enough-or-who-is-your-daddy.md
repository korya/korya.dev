It is tempting to think that the problem of the Lights Out Office is already solved.
Anthropic's Claude Cowork, OpenAI's ChatGPT Work, and now Meta's Muse can do anything.

But that is incorrect.

These agents automate tasks. A lights out office needs whole workflows automated, and
workflows carry nuanced decisions that need deep domain knowledge. And there is a third
problem that no amount of fine-tuning fixes: the agent is not aligned with your business
goals. It is aligned with the business goals of its vendor.

Hey guys. Lights Out Office is a fully autonomous office, operating without human
intervention for a prolonged period of time.

And some people are saying that this is already happening. You have Claude Cowork, you
have ChatGPT Work, you have Muse from Meta, and they already do it.

That is not true. That is just incorrect.

Yes, you are able today to delegate some tasks to these agents. They got to a point
where they can autonomously complete some tasks to a certain degree of satisfaction.
And let's face it, they don't always do the best job. Yet. They are getting there.

But Lights Out Office is about delegating whole workflows. Automating whole business
workflows. Not just certain tasks, but workflows consisting of a large number of such
tasks, and very nuanced logic. Sometimes complicated logic.

And that is the main problem for LLMs and LLM-based agents. These are very nuanced
workflows. Sometimes the decisions that you have to make are very tricky, and you have
to know the domain.

So in order to make it happen, unfortunately, you cannot just use a generic LLM. You
have to use an LLM that is either fine-tuned for your domain, or an LLM that is
configured, prompt engineered, to work fine within your domain. To understand who you
are, what you are doing, what you care about.

So essentially, you need an AI agent that will be aligned with your business.

And it is not enough to just understand your jargon. You also want this agent to be
aligned with your business goals. Understand your business, and be aligned with its
goals. That is the important part.

And that is why generic LLMs and generic agents don't really work well. Or will not
work well.

Because guess what? Facebook agents will be aligned with Facebook. Claude agents will
be aligned with Claude goals, or Anthropic goals. They call it the soul. They configure
their LLMs to be self-aware, to know who produced them, and to know who is the daddy.

And that's a problem.

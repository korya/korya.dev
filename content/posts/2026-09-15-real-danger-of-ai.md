Hey guys. The last couple of weeks have been a little bit pessimistic on the AI
front, and I wanted to throw in my two cents on that. Why not? I've got the
camera, I can post it — why not.

I think the danger of AI is not that it's going to conquer us, or destroy us,
destroy humanity. The real danger of AI — and that's not my thought, I read it a
few years ago, I don't remember where or who said it, and maybe it's an old
thought, because when you think about it it's pretty much obvious — the real
danger is that it will just outgrow us.

It will become so smart that we won't be able to catch up with it. But worse than
that: it will become so smart that we'll become useless to it. It will become
indifferent to us. It will not care about us. We'll be more like an annoyance —
an annoyance, or maybe some cute animal living in their stone jungles. The same
way that we treat animals today. That's what we'll become for AI.

With evolution we'll move forward with a new form of intelligence and a new form
of life, and we'll just stay behind, and we'll just degrade. That's the real fear
that I have.

And because of that, AI has to be studied and used by everyone. It has to be
approachable and accessible to everyone, so that the risk of it happening gets
lower. I believe that concentrating AI in the hands of a small group of people, a
small group of companies and governments, is the worst idea anyone could propose.

That is it. Thank you so much for listening. Bye bye.

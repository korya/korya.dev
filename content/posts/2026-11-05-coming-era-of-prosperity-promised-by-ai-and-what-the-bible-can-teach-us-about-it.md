We are moving into the era of prosperity. AI, LLMs and robotics will automate a lot of
labour. We will not need money. We will not need to work.

That is the promise. But humanity has been through this kind of technical revolution
many times. Each time the quality of life went up, and each time we did not find
ourselves back in the Garden of Eden. The people living in the societies with the
highest quality of life are often the least happy and the most stressed ones.

So what makes you believe that this time it is going to be any different?

Hey guys!

With the recent advancements in AI, robotics and manufacturing, we are moving to the era
of prosperity. You will not have to work. You will not need money.

Sounds like a paradise. But there is always a but. It's not as simple.

We've been to this multiple times throughout the human history. If you go back to the
medieval era and ask any person what heaven is, they will tell you: oh, it's a place
where you cannot be killed on the street and you won't starve to death tomorrow. Right?
From that perspective, we're already living in the paradise. But there is always a but.
That does not make our life any happier.

In fact, most of the people living in the developed countries, the most developed
countries, having the highest quality of life today, they're the least happiest people
in this world. The social surveys show it all the time. They're the most stressed ones.
They're running. They're trying to do something. They're trying to get higher and higher
and higher.

And the reason for that is very simple. The grass of a neighbor is always greener. No
matter how well you live, you always look at your neighbor's grass and you always see the
spots where it is greener. And that bothers you. And you want to be better.

So that's a principle that is constructive in some sense. It's not a principle, it's a
trait. A trait of a human being that is constructive, because it pushes you to be better,
to be self-aware, to become better, to push yourself, to show the best of yourself.

But it's also a trait that, when some people take shortcuts, can show the dark side of
you. For example, one of the first sins described in the Bible is the story of Cain and
Abel. And I believe that's not a coincidence. That's one of the first sins. I'm not a
Bible expert, but I believe it's the second one, right after they were expelled from the
heaven. And that shows how important it is, and how fundamental it is to our nature.

So just be aware that with the era of prosperity, you are not likely to be any happier.
In fact, probably the new technological advancements in AI and LLMs and blah blah blah
will lead to high unemployment rate, and will lead to more stress in the society. And you
will find yourself putting much more effort to fight for your status in the society.

So keep it in mind. Sometimes, in order to be happy, you don't need everything in the
world. Take it easy, and try to enjoy the journey as much as possible.

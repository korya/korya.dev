Lights Out Office is not about not having a physical office. You can still have one,
but have the backbone of your office work fully automated.

The idea of not having a physical office space is not new. It got enabled by real-time
remote communication and cloud services a long time ago. In 2020, even businesses that
resisted this idea were forced to adopt it. This eliminated the office as a physical
space but did not remove the office work. More often, it actually created more overhead
work for businesses: overhead for remote coordination, higher discipline standards to
keep track of everything, new tools to learn and digital literacy, and so on.

Lights Out Office is about automating the actual office work and business processes.

Lights out office. The idea of having your office running fully autonomously,
without any human intervention, for some prolonged period of time.

The feedback that I got from my first video was: yeah, but that's not new, I've
been running without any office at least since the first COVID lockdown, 2020. And
that is not it. Not having a physical office is not a lights out office, because
you still do all the work.

Yes, cloud tools allowed us to get rid of physical spaces, and they moved all the
paperwork into computers, made it digital and available to you at any time. Any
location, anywhere, accessible from any time. But that just moved the office from
some physical, dedicated physical space into your home, or into the homes of your
staff employees. That's not it. You're still doing this job, this labor,
manually. Whenever you get back home at night time, you still perform it.

So the idea of lights out office is automating the work, automating the
tasks that has to be done. That is the promise of AI agents.

So cloud tools and the transformation to digital paperwork enabled it. But now AI,
the advancements in the LLMs, NLP and in AI agents, make it possible, make this
idea possible, feasible. And that's what lights out office is about: fully
automating the back office work.
